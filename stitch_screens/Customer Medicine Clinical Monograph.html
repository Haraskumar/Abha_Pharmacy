Section A

Indications & Clinical Dosage

Paracetamol is indicated for the treatment of mild-to-moderate somatic and visceral pain states including tension headache, dental pain, dysmenorrhea, acute musculoskeletal discomfort, and as an antipyretic for temporary fever reduction associated with upper respiratory and viral infections.

Section B

Critical Precautions & Warnings

warning
Hepatotoxicity Alert & Contraindications

Absolute Contraindication: Severe hepatic impairment, acute active liver disease, or documented hypersensitivity to paracetamol.

  • Chronic Alcoholism: Daily consumption of ≥ 3 standard alcoholic units markedly amplifies liver damage risks from therapeutic doses.
  • Persistent Symptoms: Consult a qualified physician or pharmacist immediately if fever persists beyond 3 days or pain persists beyond 5 days.
  • Emergency Threshold: Accidental ingestion exceeding 4000mg requires immediate medical evaluation and administration of N-acetylcysteine (NAC) regardless of symptoms.
Section C

Administration & Advice

local_drink
Ingestion Protocol

Swallow whole with a full glass of water. Can be taken with or without food. Taking with meals may reduce potential minor gastric irritation.

block
Polypharmacy Check

Do not combine with any cold syrups, sinus capsules, or prescription analgesics that also contain Acetaminophen / APAP.
